PitchPal

Note by note, build your sound.

Plays a note, listens to you sing it back, and shows you with a floating dot whether you're too high, too low, or nailed it.

How the plan grew from scope

Scope said: "it plays a note → you sing it → you adjust until the dot hits the line."

Now we know exactly what that means: the note plays for a few seconds, you get 3 tries of ~5 seconds, a hit means holding the note on the line for 3 seconds, silence doesn't cost a try, and after three misses you choose Try again or Skip.

The core journey

Features and how we'll know they work

Opening and Home scope › The Core Loop
  • Opening shows "PitchPal" + "Note by note, build your sound." for a few seconds, then Home appears with no tap.
  • Home shows "Listen", "Sing it back", "Watch the dot" and a Start button.
  • Pressing Start begins a 5-note round.
Playing the note
  • Each note plays out loud for a few seconds.
  • The screen makes clear when to listen and when it's your turn to sing.
Live pitch feedback (the dot) scope › The Unique Kernel
  • The dot moves up when you go higher and down when you go lower, in real time.
  • Above the target: dot above the line + "too high". Below: dot below + "too low".
  • The further off you are, the further the dot sits from the line.
Hitting the note
  • Holding the note on the line for at least 3 seconds turns the dot green and shows "You nailed it, well done!"
  • Briefly passing through the line (a wobble) does not count.
  • "On the line" is a fixed zone, in between and leaning forgiving, the same every round.
  • A hit is recorded as a pass and the next note begins.
Tries, retry and skip
  • 3 tries of ~5 seconds per note, and the screen shows which try you're on.
  • After 3 misses: a missed-all-three message + encouragement + Try again / Skip.
  • Try again = same note, 3 fresh tries. A hit then still counts as a pass.
  • Skip moves on. The skipped note counts as a miss.
Note name and Hear it again added in 4-spec
  • Shows the note with its number, e.g. "C4", plus a hint on every note saying what its number means (e.g. 3 = a little below the middle of the piano).
  • Hear it again replays the note and restarts the try without using one up. No limit.
  • The right note an octave higher or lower counts.
Pick a note added in 4-spec
  • A Pick a note button on Home → 8 white keys, C4 to C5.
  • Tap a key, hear it, sing it: same dot, 3 tries, 3-second hold, Hear it again, Try again / Skip.
  • Running score, e.g. "4 nailed out of 6 notes". Back shows it, then Home. Nothing saved.
  • Built after the 5-note round works.
Leaving a round early added in 4-spec
  • A small Back button on the Practice screen.
  • It pauses and asks: "Leave this round? Your progress won't be saved." with Keep practicing / Leave.
  • Leave → Home, progress discarded. Keep practicing → restarts the current try.
End-of-round score scope › The Core Loop
  • After 5 notes: passes out of 5, e.g. "3/5 on pitch".
  • A short line on which way you mostly missed, e.g. "mostly sang low"
  • You can play another round or go Home.
Theme toggle scope › The POC Boundary
  • A toggle switches light ↔ dark, both matching the look below.

What if…

Microphone refused

A message explains the microphone is needed to hear you sing. No round starts.

Silence during a try

I can't hear you, try singing louder. The silent try doesn't count against your 3.

Three misses

Missed-all-three message, encouragement, then Try again or Skip.

Leaving mid-round

Back asks Leave this round? Your progress won't be saved. Leave goes Home; Keep practicing returns to the round.

Closing the app

Nothing is saved. The score lives only for the round, by design.
